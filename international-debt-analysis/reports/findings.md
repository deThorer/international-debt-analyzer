# Key findings

_Generated by `src/run_analysis.py` from `international_debt_sample.csv`._

> **Warning: these numbers come from the SYNTHETIC sample file.** Run the pipeline on the real dataset before quoting any figure.

- The data covers **120 countries** and **25 debt indicators**.
- Summing every indicator gives **US$ 2.17 trillion**, but **60%** of that is double counting (parent series include their children). The de-duplicated total is **US$ 0.87 trillion**.
- **China** is the largest debtor: US$ 202.22 bn (US$ 86.51 bn de-duplicated), followed by Brazil and Russian Federation.
- The indicator with the highest average is `DT.DIS.DLXF.CD` (Disbursements on external debt, long-term (DIS, current US$)), US$ 3,454 million on average.
- Largest long-term principal repayment: **China** (US$ 40.52 bn).
- Long-term principal repayments by creditor: Private (PPG) 41%, Official (PPG) 28%, Private non-guaranteed 26%.
- 2 of the 3 indicators with the highest averages are long-term (DLXF) series, and they are the only series that can be summed without double counting. **Recommendation:** use long-term debt as the headline measure and break it down by creditor type, instead of adding up all 25 indicators.
