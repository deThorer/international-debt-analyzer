# Key findings

_Generated by `src/run_analysis.py` from `international_debt.csv`._

- The data covers **121 countries** and **25 debt indicators**.
- Summing every indicator gives **US$ 2.44 trillion**, but **60%** of that is double counting (parent series include their children). The de-duplicated total is **US$ 0.97 trillion**.
- **China** is the largest debtor: US$ 285.79 bn (US$ 129.78 bn de-duplicated), followed by Brazil and Russian Federation.
- The indicator with the highest average is `DT.AMT.DLXF.CD` (Principal repayments on external debt, long-term (AMT, current US$)), US$ 5,271 million on average.
- Largest long-term principal repayment: **China** (US$ 96.22 bn).
- Long-term principal repayments by creditor: Private non-guaranteed 58%, Private (PPG) 25%, Official (PPG) 16%.
- One of the 3 indicators with the highest averages is a long-term (DLXF) series, and long-term series are the only ones that can be summed without double counting. **Recommendation:** use long-term debt as the headline measure and break it down by creditor type, instead of adding up all 25 indicators.
